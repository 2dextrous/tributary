# Test script: 10 minutes, 5 people

Run it twice: on the live page now, and later on the style tiles and the new build. Use people from the audience: two recruiters or hiring managers, two supply-chain or operations people, and one analyst or engineer. Each uses their own phone, ideally on a video call with their screen shared, so you can see where they tap.

Before you start, say: "I'm testing the page, not you. Please think out loud. There are no wrong answers." Ask before recording.

## 1. Five-second test (1 minute)

Send the link and say "Open it, look for five seconds, then put the phone face down." Then ask:

- What is this?
- Who is it for?
- What would you do first?

**Pass:** they describe planning a supply chain, network or warehouses (any words) and name something to do.

## 2. First click (1 minute)

"Diesel prices go up 20%. Where would you tap first to see what that does to the business?" Note the first thing they tap.

**Pass:** the freight lever, a diesel scenario button, or the copilot.

## 3. Think aloud: a cost shock (3 minutes)

"Find out what happens to the yearly cost, and to the warehouses, if freight costs rise sharply."

**Pass:** within 60 seconds they make the change and say the cost went up. Note whether they notice that the network changed.

## 4. Think aloud: a plant outage (3 minutes)

"A plant shuts for a month. Do customers still get their goods?"

**Pass:** they set an outage (Simulate tab or a copilot scenario) and read off the effect on service (fill rate).

## 5. Wrap-up (2 minutes)

- "Overall, how easy was that, from 1 (very hard) to 7 (very easy)?"
- "Give me three words that describe how it looks."
- "Would you show it to a colleague? Why or why not?"

## For the style tiles (Phase 2)

Show the three tiles in a different order for each person: ABC, BCA, CAB, ACB, BAC. Run the five-second test on each tile, then ask "Which one would you most like to use, and why?"
